## How It Works

**1. Intelligent Company Profiling**

The platform begins by understanding who you are. Not just your industry—but your company's financial character: size, ownership structure, financial health, margin profile, leverage situation. A highly leveraged PE-backed company has different priorities than a family-owned business with strong margins and no debt.

This profile drives everything that follows. Benchmarks, priorities, and recommendations are calibrated to a company like yours—not a generic template.

**2. Context-Aware Benchmarking**

Based on your profile, the platform sets the right benchmarks for your situation. What "good" looks like for a $50M company is different from a $500M company. What matters for a cash-constrained business is different from one with healthy liquidity. The platform understands this context and calibrates accordingly.

**3. Structured Diagnostic Assessment**

Targeted questions across 26 FP&A practices, organized into 9 objectives under 3 themes. Each question maps to exactly one practice—no ambiguity, no overlap. Your answers feed into sophisticated AI-driven algorithms that work in the background: weighting factors, identifying patterns, surfacing priorities—all with proper guardrails to keep the assessment rigorous and auditable.

**4. The Three-Stage Maturity Journey**

The diagnostic maps your FP&A function against a clear maturity progression:

- **The Foundation** (Control & Trust) — Can leadership trust your numbers? Do budgets hold?
- **The Future** (Speed & Agility) — Can you forecast quickly and adapt when plans change?
- **The Intelligence** (Value & Influence) — Are you a strategic partner or just a reporting function?

Each theme breaks down into specific practices that map to real operational capabilities. The assessment shows you where you stand on this journey—and what it takes to move forward.

**5. Gap & Risk Identification**

The platform compares your current state against the benchmarks for a company like yours. It surfaces the gaps between where you are and where you should be, flags critical risks that need immediate attention, and identifies opportunities for improvement.

Every gap gets tagged by root cause across eight dimensions: People, Process, Technology, Data, Governance, Culture, Risk, Communication. This matters because the path forward for a technology gap is radically different from a culture gap. The platform doesn't just show you the gap—it helps you understand why it exists.

**6. Scoring with Guard Rails**

The scoring engine is "Fair but Firm." High scores in easy areas don't mask critical gaps elsewhere. If your planning process needs work, that shows up clearly—even if your reporting is excellent. Critical gates prevent misleading maturity levels.

**7. War Room & Initiative Engine**

This is the core of the platform. Your diagnostic results feed directly into a prioritized initiative list, scored by Impact and Complexity. High-impact, low-complexity items surface first. Critical questions carry 2× multipliers.

Select an initiative, commit to a timeline (6, 12, or 24 months), and see your projected maturity shift instantly. You're not staring at a static report—you're building a sequenced transformation roadmap you can defend.

**8. AI Interpretation (Not AI Grading)**

The platform uses AI to generate narrative explanations of your results—executive summaries, practice-level insights, talking points for leadership. But the AI never changes your scores. It explains; it doesn't grade. The assessment stays deterministic and auditable.

**9. Executive Report**

Once you finalize your action plan in the War Room, the platform generates a **polished, board-ready Executive Report**—the document you hand to the CEO or board.

The report includes your company profile and context, current maturity assessment against relevant benchmarks, identified gaps and critical risks, the sequenced roadmap with committed timelines, projected improvements, and AI-generated narrative that explains the "why" behind your priorities.

This isn't a screenshot of a dashboard. It's a professional deliverable that positions you as strategic—not reactive. You walk away equipped.
