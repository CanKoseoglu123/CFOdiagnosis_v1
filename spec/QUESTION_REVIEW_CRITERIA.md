# Question Review Criteria

**Version:** 1.0 | **Updated:** 2025-01-10

---

| # | Criterion | Pass If... | ❌ Fail Example | ✅ Pass Example |
|---|-----------|------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| 1 | **Binary Clarity** | CFO can answer Yes/No without "it depends" | "Is budgeting effective?" | "Does the company produce an approved annual budget before the fiscal year begins?" |
| 2 | **Maturity Fit** | L1→L4 progression within practice makes sense | L2 question assumes L3 capability exists | L1: budget exists → L2: budget has process owner → L3: rolling reforecasts → L4: continuous planning |
| 3 | **Diagnostic Value** | "No" exposes a real gap | "Does Finance use spreadsheets?" (Yes tells you nothing) | "Can every line of controllable OpEx be mapped to a specific human owner?" |
| 4 | **Why It Matters** | Help text explains *so what* | "This is a best practice." | "Without sign-off, sales leaders can claim targets were imposed rather than committed to." |
| 5 | **Plain Language** | Non-finance exec understands it | "Is there adequate CoA granularity for OPEX segmentation?" | "Can you track spending separately by department, project, and cost type?" |
| 6 | **Logical Sequence** | Earlier questions don't assume later ones | Asking about forecast accuracy before asking if forecasts exist | Asking if budget exists before asking about budget process owner |
| 7 | **No Redundancy** | Each question tests something distinct | Two questions both asking about budget ownership with different wording | One question on OpEx ownership, separate question on CapEx ownership |
| 8 | **Critical Flag Validity** | "No" should block Green for the practice | Marking "AI-powered narratives" as critical | Marking "approved annual budget exists" as critical |
| 9 | **Impact/Complexity Calibration** | Scores reflect effort vs. payoff | Impact 5 for a cosmetic reporting change | Impact 5 for single data source (eliminates reconciliation chaos) |
| 10 | **Level-Appropriate Scope** | L1 achievable by any company; L4 genuinely advanced | L1: "Continuous close capabilities" | L1: "Budget exists"; L4: "Continuous close capabilities" |
